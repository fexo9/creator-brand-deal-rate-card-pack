# CONTRACT CLAUSE CHECKLIST
## Verify Every Clause Before You Sign — check each box or walk away

**How to use this checklist:** Run through it for every brand deal, no matter how small or how friendly the contact is. A missing clause is a future argument. If a clause is absent from their draft, ask for it in writing — use the closing email (file 04, Email 4) to confirm additions.

**Note on legal advice:** This checklist helps you spot issues — it is not legal advice. For deals above {your threshold, e.g. $2,000} or any clause you don't fully understand, have a lawyer review the contract.

---

## PAYMENT

- [ ] **Fee amount** stated as an exact number in an exact currency (no "TBD," no "to be discussed").
- [ ] **Payment schedule** defined: {e.g. "50% within 7 days of signing, 50% within 7 days of posting"}.
- [ ] **Payment method** named: {bank transfer / PayPal / platform}.
- [ ] **Late-payment term** included: {e.g. "1.5% per month after 30 days overdue"}.
- [ ] **Who pays fees/taxes** stated (transfer fees, VAT — don't assume).

**Red flag:** "Payment within 90 days of posting" or payment tied to campaign performance metrics you don't control.

---

## SCOPE OF WORK

- [ ] **Exact deliverables** listed: {e.g. "1× 60s TikTok video + 2× Instagram stories"}.
- [ ] **Posting platform(s)** named — one platform is not all platforms.
- [ ] **Posting date or window** agreed: {e.g. "between {Date1} and {Date2}"}.
- [ ] **Content stays live** for a defined period: {e.g. "minimum 12 months, no deletion"}.
- [ ] **Revision limit** capped: {e.g. "2 rounds included; extra rounds at ${Price} each"}.
- [ ] **Approval process** defined: {e.g. "brand approves script within 3 business days; silence = approval"}.

**Red flag:** "Creator will produce content as requested by brand" with no limits — that's unlimited revisions in disguise.

---

## USAGE RIGHTS

- [ ] **Organic posting rights** (your own feed) — standard, should always be included.
- [ ] **Paid-usage rights** (brand runs your content as ads): duration capped — {e.g. "30/60/90 days max"}, platforms named, fee stated separately.
- [ ] **No perpetual usage** unless the fee reflects it (perpetual = priced like a buyout, not a post).
- [ ] **Whitelisting** (ads run from YOUR handle): separate fee, separate duration cap.
- [ ] **Repurposing** (brand re-edits your footage): requires written permission, priced separately.
- [ ] **Usage renewal** priced in advance: {e.g. "+{X}% per additional 30 days"}.

**Red flag:** "Brand may use content in perpetuity across all media" buried in the boilerplate.

---

## EXCLUSIVITY

- [ ] **Category** defined narrowly: {e.g. "protein powder," not "all supplements"}.
- [ ] **Duration** capped: {e.g. "30/60/90 days"} — longer needs a retainer, not a one-off fee.
- [ ] **Exclusivity fee** stated separately from the post fee.
- [ ] **Start date** of exclusivity defined (posting date, not signing date).
- [ ] **Carve-outs** listed: {e.g. "existing affiliate links may remain"}.

**Red flag:** 6–12 month category exclusivity for the price of one post.

---

## KILL FEE & CANCELLATION

- [ ] **Kill fee** if the brand cancels after you've started work: {e.g. "50% after filming, 100% after posting"}.
- [ ] **Your cancellation right** stated: {e.g. "creator may cancel with {X} days' notice before filming"}.
- [ ] **Brand delay clause:** if the brand stalls approval past {X} days, the kill fee or a rescheduling fee applies.
- [ ] **Deposit non-refundable** once work begins.

---

## FTC / AD-DISCLOSURE COMPLIANCE

- [ ] **Disclosure requirement** acknowledged by both sides: content will be labeled as advertising (e.g. #ad, "Paid partnership," platform branded-content tools).
- [ ] **Responsibility** assigned: {who ensures the disclosure is present and correct}.
- [ ] ⚠️ **Verify locally:** disclosure rules, required wording, and penalties vary by country (FTC in the US, ASA in the UK, and equivalents elsewhere). Check the rules where YOU and the BRAND operate before signing.

**Red flag:** A brand asking you to hide the sponsorship or "keep it subtle" — decline in writing.

---

## FINAL PRE-SIGNING CHECKS

- [ ] All blanks filled — no "TBD," no empty exhibits, no referenced documents you haven't seen.
- [ ] The brand's legal entity name and signatory are real and verifiable.
- [ ] You have a countersigned copy, not just your own signature page.
- [ ] Key dates (posting, payment, usage expiry, exclusivity end) are in your calendar.
- [ ] The math matches the Rate Calculator (file 01): fee ÷ views = your agreed per-1k rate.

**Golden rule:** The contract you sign is the deal you have. Verbal promises, friendly DMs, and "we always do it this way" are not in the contract — so they are not in the deal.
